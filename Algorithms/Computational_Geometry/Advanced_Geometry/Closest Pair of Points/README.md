## Overview

**Closest Pair of Points** is a divide and conquer algorithm that finds the closest pair of points in a 2D plane.

**Time Complexity:** O(n log n)  
**Space Complexity:** O(n)

---

## Why It's in Divide and Conquer?

This algorithm is categorized under Divide and Conquer because:
- It uses the divide and conquer paradigm
- It's a classic example of divide and conquer algorithms
- The implementation follows divide and conquer principles

However, it's also relevant to Computational Geometry, which is why it's referenced here.
